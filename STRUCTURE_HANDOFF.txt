1. Final repo shape
==================================================

Top-level structure now emphasizes runtime vs research vs deployment:

- `services/`
  - `eep/` - public orchestration entrypoint scaffold
  - `segmentation/` - segmentation service
  - `measurements/` - measurement-stage runtime code, including integrated Group 5 runtime modules
  - `interpretation/` - Group 6 thresholding / interpretation stage
  - `reporting/` - report-generation scaffold
- `research/`
  - `group5/` - Group 5 validation scripts, exploratory methods, benchmarks, and research-only tests
- `colab/`
  - `group5/` - GPU notebooks used to generate segmentation artifacts outside local runtime
- `deployment/`
  - `docker/`, `compose/`, `k8s/` - deployment scaffolds
- `docs/`
  - architecture, rubric, validation, deployment, tradeoff, and demo notes
- `tests/`
  - `integration/`, `e2e/` - top-level homes for cross-service and full-pipeline testing
