\subsection{Group 3 --- canal/cord/SAC: strong separation}
Canal-AP minimum and SAC minimum both separate at $p=0.0001$; the distributions barely touch (healthy
canal floor \SI{10.5}{\milli\meter} vs.\ unhealthy ceiling \SI{9.97}{\milli\meter}). Cord AP is thinner in
the symptomatic cohort ($p=0.009$). This is the headline: on real MRI, the canal/cord measurements read
normal on healthy necks and cross into stenosis on a CSM/CSR cohort --- exactly the disease this cohort
carries.

\subsection{Group 4 --- Cobb C1: correct method, directional discrimination}
C1 reads healthy \SI{+15.2}{\degree} (proper lordosis, matching the literature mean \SI{15.4}{\degree})
vs.\ unhealthy \SI{+8.8}{\degree} (straightened). The direction is correct and C1 is far cleaner than the
legacy canal-cut method (which produced a \SI{+56}{\degree} healthy outlier). The separation is not
significant at this $n$ ($p=0.13$): alignment is inherently less specific for myelopathy than canal
narrowing, and $n$ is small. C1 is validated as a \emph{method}; as a discriminator it is directional.

\subsection{Group 1 --- compression: correctly null}
No difference (0.81 vs.\ 0.80, $p=0.92$), zero compression flags in either cohort. This is the expected
and correct outcome: spondylosis is not compression fracture, so vertebral heights are normal (true
negatives). It empirically confirms that this cohort does not exercise the compression axis (which needs a
dedicated fracture dataset) and re-confirms the Group 5.2 specificity on all 12 healthy controls.

\subsection{Group 2 --- disc: the harness catches a bug}
Both disc metrics point the wrong way: DHI is lower in healthy (0.23) than unhealthy (0.26), and bulge is
higher in healthy (2.95\,mm) than unhealthy (0.91\,mm). A metric that scores healthy worse than pathology
is invalid. The DHI denominator (adjacent-VB middle height) is over-measured at the cervicothoracic
junction (Section~\ref{sec:g2}), collapsing DHI. Group 2 is not validated; the root cause is documented
and flagged. That the validation \emph{detected} this is itself a positive result for the methodology.

\subsection{Overall}
Four of five groups behave correctly: G3 strongly validated, G4-C1 method-validated/directional, G1
correctly null, G5 validated (prior runs). Our own methods required no fixes --- they passed or were
correctly null --- which independently validates the earlier development work (Section~\ref{sec:journey}).
The one failure (G2) is a teammate module with a root-caused, flagged bug.
